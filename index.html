At Think Differently Dyslexia Support, we provide professional diagnostic dyslexia assessments to help identify a learner’s strengths, challenges, and whether their literacy difficulties are consistent with dyslexia. Working closely with families and schools, we deliver clear answers, recognised reports, and practical recommendations to help every learner move forward with confidence.

Our Services

Clear Answers Through
Dyslexia Assessment

Whether you're seeking a first indication through our Dyslexia Screening Assessment or a fully recognised Diagnostic Assessment, we offer professional services tailored to each learner. Every assessment is evidence-based, child-centred, and designed to provide clear outcomes and practical next steps for home, school, and future education.
