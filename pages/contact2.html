A Dyslexia Screening Assessment (£100, around an hour, with verbal feedback) is a helpful first step if you're unsure whether a full assessment is needed. The Full Diagnostic Assessment (£480, approximately 3 hours) provides a formal diagnosis and a detailed written report recognised by schools.

Assessments focus on dyslexia and related literacy difficulties. Indicators of other challenges, such as attention or memory difficulties, may be noted, but conditions like ADHD, autism or dyscalculia are not formally diagnosed — the report can, however, highlight areas for further exploration.
