About Me

Clear Answers, Delivered
With Care

I have worked in education for over 20 years, across mainstream classrooms and specialist one-to-one teaching. As a qualified specialist teacher and dyslexia assessor, I have seen how literacy difficulties can affect far more than just schoolwork — they can impact confidence, self-esteem, and a child's overall experience of learning.

I created Think Differently Dyslexia Support because I wanted families to have access to clear, honest, and professional support when they are worried about their child's progress. Many parents feel overwhelmed trying to understand why their child is struggling, what school support is available, and whether dyslexia may be part of the picture. My goal is to make that process feel much clearer and less stressful.

I provide thorough diagnostic assessments that help identify a learner's strengths and difficulties, offering practical recommendations and recognised reports that support the next steps both in school and beyond. Just as importantly, I want every family to feel listened to, supported, and confident moving forward.

Our Approach

Assessment Philosophy
& Core Values

A good assessment is about far more than testing. It is about building trust, putting a child at ease, and giving families answers they can actually act on.

Every assessment at Think Differently is built around three principles: clarity, reassurance, and practical guidance. Concepts and results are explained with absolute clarity, children are supported to feel comfortable throughout, and every report ends with recommendations families and schools can put into practice.

I stay up to date with current assessment standards and educational research, so every family receives the most accurate and effective support available.
